## Procedural geometry
The remaining standing objects (table, barrel, kiosk, bench, stool, bookshelf)
and all overhead objects (cables, lanterns, signs) are built from three.js
primitives (BoxGeometry, CylinderGeometry, SphereGeometry, TubeGeometry).
